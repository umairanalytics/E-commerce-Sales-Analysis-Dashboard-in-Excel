# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, profitability, product performance, and regional trends.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data from **2011 to 2014** to identify important business trends and generate actionable insights.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into interactive visualizations and KPIs covering sales, profit, quantity, orders, product categories, sub-categories, and geographic performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Analyze overall sales and profitability
* Identify sales and profit trends over time
* Compare product categories and sub-categories
* Identify top-performing products
* Analyze regional and state-level performance
* Identify areas generating high sales but low or negative profit
* Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Sales Performance

Sales increased significantly during the four-year period, reaching approximately **$733.95K in 2014**, compared with approximately **$484.25K in 2011**.

### Category Performance

**Technology** was the highest-performing category by sales, generating approximately **$836.15K** in revenue and approximately **$145.45K** in profit.

### Product Performance

**Phones** and **Chairs** were among the strongest sub-categories by sales.

### Profitability

Although some products and regions generated high sales, they did not necessarily generate high profits.

For example, **Tables generated significant sales but resulted in an overall loss of approximately $17.73K**, highlighting an opportunity to investigate pricing, discounts, and costs.

### Regional Performance

**California** and **New York** were among the strongest states by sales.

At the same time, some high-sales states, including **Texas, Pennsylvania, and Ohio**, showed negative profitability.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. Focus on high-performing categories such as Technology.
2. Investigate loss-making sub-categories such as Tables.
3. Analyze pricing and discount strategies for products with high sales but low profit.
4. Investigate states generating high revenue but negative profit.
5. Continue monitoring year-over-year sales and profitability trends.
